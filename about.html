About Garud Constructions

The name Garud represents vision, strength, and unwavering reliability. For nearly two decades, we have focused on constructing homes, roads, and commercial buildings right here in our local area.

Our Heritage

Building Trust in Every Neighborhood

Garud Constructions was started with a straightforward vision: to give local property owners and housing societies a contractor they could look in the eye and trust. Too many construction projects suffer from runaway costs, poor cement mixes, or disappearing contractors.

At Garud, our owner and civil supervisors are personally present on site every day. Whether we are pouring foundation pillars for a family bungalow, rolling asphalt for an internal colony road, or casting the roof slab of a commercial shopping complex, we maintain strict quality control.

Our Promise to Every Client

"Only certified 53-grade cement, TMT Fe-550D steel, clean river sand, and written milestone agreements. No hidden surcharges, no delays."
