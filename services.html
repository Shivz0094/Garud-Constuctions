Homes, Roads & Building Solutions

From casting individual dream houses to building municipal roads and multi-story commercial plazas, Garud Constructions provides complete turnkey execution with top-quality materials.

We specialize in custom independent bungalow and duplex house construction for local families. We manage the entire lifecycle: soil testing, foundation, RCC pillars, red brick / fly ash masonry, slab casting, electrical wiring, plumbing, plastering, and premium elevation finish.

Turnkey House Packages

Both with-material and labor contracts with transparent per-sq-ft rates.

Earthquake-Resistant RCC

Specialization 02

Roads, Paving & Drainage Works

Our in-house equipment fleet enables us to execute rapid asphalt road paving, concrete road construction (PQC / DLC), interlocking paver block driveways, and storm water side drainage for residential societies and commercial premises.

Bitumen / Tar Asphalt Roads: Graded stone aggregate, hot mix bitumen laying, and heavy vibratory rolling for zero water seepage.
Heavy-Duty Concrete Paver Blocks: 60mm to 80mm M-35/M-40 grade paver blocks with edge kerb stones and proper sub-base sand bedding.
RCC Box Drains & Culverts: Integrated drainage prevents monsoon flooding and extends road life.
