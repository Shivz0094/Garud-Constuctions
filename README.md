# Garud Constructions - Local Construction Platform

A modern, high-converting, mobile-responsive website customized for **Garud Constructions**, focusing on local **Home Building**, **Roads & Pavement Infrastructure**, and **Commercial Building Construction**.

---

## 🦅 Brand & Design Overhaul

### 1. New Company Identity & Tagline
- **Company Name**: **Garud Constructions**
- **Brand Symbol**: Garud (Eagle motif) representing strength, vision, and rock-solid foundation.
- **Tagline**: *"Homes • Roads • Buildings | Building Your Vision From the Ground Up"*
- **Local Focus**: Tailored specifically for local area homeowners, societies, and commercial businesses.

### 2. Dark & Light Mode Switcher
- Persistent theme toggle button with animated Sun & Moon icons in the top navigation header and mobile drawer.
- Stores preference in `localStorage` across page visits.
- **Dark Mode**: Rich navy slate (`#0a1322` & `#0f1e36`) with safety amber/orange accents.
- **Light Mode**: Crisp clean white background with concrete contrast borders and bold industrial typography.

### 3. WhatsApp Chat Integration
- **Floating WhatsApp Button**: Positioned at the bottom-right corner with a continuous pulsating wave animation.
- **Interactive Tooltip**: *"Chat with Garud Team on WhatsApp"*.
- **Direct Link**: Opens WhatsApp with prefilled inquiries (`https://wa.me/919876543210`) for immediate mobile quote requests.
- Additional WhatsApp quick-chat triggers in the header and mobile action bar.

### 4. Specialization Shift (Homes, Roads & Buildings)
- **Home Construction & Bungalows**: Custom independent houses, duplex villas, RCC pillar casting, turnkey material + labor contracts.
- **Roads & Pavement Works**: Asphalt/tar carpet laying, 80mm heavy-duty concrete paver blocks for residential societies, storm water drains, vibratory compaction.
- **Building & Commercial Construction**: G+1 to G+5 apartment buildings, shopping plazas, retail shopfronts, and structural renovations.
- **Localized Cost Estimator**: Calculates realistic regional costs in ₹ (INR) with separate rates for Homes, Roads, Buildings, and Renovation.
